# Dead by Daylight — Asymmetric Multiplayer Horror

---

## What Is Dead by Daylight?

Dead by Daylight is an asymmetric multiplayer horror game developed by Behaviour Interactive. Each match brings together one Killer and four Survivors in a tense contest built around pursuit, stealth, teamwork, and escape.

Players can experience both sides of the hunt. Killers track Survivors and attempt to stop their escape, while Survivors cooperate to repair generators, avoid detection, rescue teammates, and reach an exit. Each role has different objectives, abilities, and tactical considerations.

The game features a changing roster of original characters and licensed figures from horror films, television, and other entertainment franchises. Different characters introduce distinct powers, perks, and play styles, encouraging players to experiment with new strategies.

Maps, objectives, character abilities, and player decisions create unpredictable matches. Success depends on reading the situation, managing risk, and adapting tactics as the hunt develops.

## Core Game Features

### Killer Gameplay

Playing as the Killer means tracking Survivors, applying pressure across the map, and preventing the team from completing its objectives. Each Killer has a distinct power that shapes how they locate, chase, or obstruct opponents.

Some powers reward accurate timing, while others focus on area control, stealth, tracking, or controlling routes. Learning the strengths and limitations of each Killer helps players choose tactics suited to different maps and Survivor strategies.

### Survivor Teamwork

Survivors must cooperate to repair generators and activate an escape route while avoiding the Killer. Teammates can distract the hunter, rescue captured players, heal injuries, and coordinate their movements to improve the team's chances.

Every decision involves risk. Repairing an objective may reveal a Survivor's position, while attempting a rescue can expose another teammate to danger. Effective teamwork requires awareness of the match situation and the condition of the entire group.

### Character Roster

Dead by Daylight includes original characters alongside licensed figures from recognizable horror franchises. Characters differ in appearance, background, abilities, and available perks.

Exploring the roster allows players to find characters that suit their preferred approach, whether they favor aggressive pursuit, stealth, information gathering, or team support.

### Perks & Character Builds

Perks modify gameplay by adding specific effects and tactical options. Depending on the selected character and role, perks can support tracking, healing, objective completion, chase tactics, or team coordination.

Choosing a combination of perks is an important part of preparation. Different combinations encourage different decisions during a match, and changing a build can help players adapt to their preferred role.

### Maps & Environmental Awareness

Matches take place across varied environments with distinct layouts, obstacles, hiding places, and objective locations. Buildings, walls, pallets, windows, and other features influence movement and pursuit.

Learning the layout of a map helps players anticipate danger, plan escape routes, and make better decisions during chases. Environmental awareness matters for both Killers and Survivors.

### Progression & Customization

The game features character development systems, unlockable perks, cosmetics, and other customization options. Players can tailor character appearances and adjust available gameplay choices as they become familiar with different roles.

The available options depend on the character, account unlocks, and current game content. New additions and balance changes can also affect how players approach familiar strategies.

## Match Strategy & Gameplay

### Chase Tactics

Chases are a central part of the game. Killers attempt to close distance and predict movement, while Survivors use obstacles and route choices to create opportunities to escape.

Successful play depends on timing, positioning, and recognizing when to continue a chase or move toward another objective. Familiarity with common map structures can improve decision-making on both sides.

### Objectives & Match Pressure

Survivors must balance generator repair with healing, rescues, and avoiding the Killer. Spending too much time on one task can give the opposing player an advantage elsewhere on the map.

Killers must decide where to apply pressure and which threats deserve immediate attention. Monitoring objectives and anticipating Survivor movement can help prevent the opposing team from gaining too much ground.

### Stealth & Information

Staying out of sight can help Survivors avoid a direct encounter, but remaining hidden for too long may slow the team's objectives. Sound cues, visual indicators, and environmental details can reveal useful information about nearby activity.

Killers also rely on information to locate opponents. Recognizing signs of movement and understanding likely Survivor routes can make tracking more effective.

### Team Support & Rescues

Helping teammates is essential to Survivor play. Healing, distracting the Killer, and rescuing captured players can keep the team active and create opportunities to complete objectives.

However, rescue attempts require judgment. Players need to consider the Killer's location, the remaining objectives, and the risk of creating another vulnerable teammate.
